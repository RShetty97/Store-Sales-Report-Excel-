# Store-Sales-Report-Excel-
This is an Excel Project that analyzes the sales of a retail store. This report provides information on various sales criteria such as 'Sales by platform', 'Sales by age group' etc. This helps understand strong and weak customer bases.

Tasks performed:

Data Cleaning:
Fixed mismatches in column 'Gender' and 'Qty'. 
Converted the case in the state and city columns to proper case

Data Processing:
Created column 'Age group' to group customers by age. 
Extracted the month into a separate column from the date column

Data Analysis
1) Created a line chart comparing sales and order amounts per month
Sales and orders were highest in March
Sales and orders were lowest during November-December
Sales and orders seem to increase during Jan-March and decrease during September-December

2) Created a pie chart contrasting sales between men and women
Women contributed to more sales (64%) compared to men (36%)

3) Created a pie chart comparing status of orders (delivered, refunded, returned and cancelled
Successful deliveries percentage = 92%

4) Created a bar chart depicting the top 5 states by sales (ordered from highest to lowest)

5) Created a column chart comparing number of orders across age groups (youth, middle aged and seniors)
Middle aged customers (aged betweeb 30 and 60) ordered the most, followed by youth (aged 30 and below) and seniors (aged 60 and below)
Women ordered more than men across age groups

6) Created a bar chart depicting sales across various platforms used by the store (ordered from highest to lowest)
